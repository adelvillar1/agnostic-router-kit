Plan quota & steering

      
No plan provider exposes a quota API (probed — no headers, no balance endpoints), so allowances are estimates: derived from your console readings through the weighted ledger, or declared outright. Off-peak hours count at their declared weight. Headroom drives steering — a tier diverts to its next candidate when the current one drops below  headroom, and only within that tier's own chain. Providers without a declaration are neutral: never steered away from.
